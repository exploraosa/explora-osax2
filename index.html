We’re Greivin and Hannah, the founders of Explora Osa.

We created Explora Osa to share our love for surfing, local adventure and the incredible natural beauty of this region while building a sense of community along the way.

From the Pacific Ocean to the rainforest, we connect travelers with experiences that feel personal, authentic and rooted in the place we call home.

Learn to surf with Greivin and Hannah at Playa Chaman. Private lessons for all levels and ages, with a focus on safety, confidence, technique and having fun in the water.

2 HOURS $70 PERSON

Our private surf lessons are designed for beginners and intermediate surfers who want personalized instruction in a relaxed environment.

  • 2-hour private lesson

OUR STORY

Two people.
One place we love.

We’re Greivin and Hannah, the founders of Explora Osa.

Greivin was born and raised in Costa Rica, in the mountains of Puriscal. He grew up surfing and exploring the ocean, forests and wildlife that make this country so special. His deep connection to the land and local knowledge is at the heart of every experience we offer.

Hannah is from the United States and grew up adventuring through the outdoors of Oregon and Colorado. She discovered surfing while backpacking and traveling, which eventually led her to Uvita in 2024.

We created Explora Osa as something we could grow together, a way to share our passion for nature, conservation and meaningful travel experiences with others.

OUR STORY

Two people. One place we love.

We're Greivin and Hannah, the founders of Explora Osa.

Greivin was born and raised in Costa Rica, in the mountains of Puriscal. He grew up surfing and exploring the ocean, forests and wildlife that make this country so special.

Hannah is from the United States and grew up adventuring through the outdoors of Oregon and Colorado. She discovered surfing while backpacking and traveling, which eventually brought her to Uvita in 2024.

We created Explora Osa as something we could grow together — a way to share our passion for nature, conservation and meaningful travel experiences with others.
